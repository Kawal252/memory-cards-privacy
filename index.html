Privacy Policy for Memory Cards

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Memory Cards ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Memory Cards does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photographs, or other personal information.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App may store game-related information locally on your device, such as game progress, scores, levels, settings, or other gameplay information required for the App to function.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

3. Internet and Third-Party Services

The current version of Memory Cards does not use advertising services, analytics services, Firebase, or other third-party services that collect personal information.

The current version of the App does not transmit personal information to external servers.

4. Advertising

The current version of Memory Cards does not display advertisements and does not contain advertising SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy

Memory Cards does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Because the App does not collect personal information, no personal information is knowingly collected from users of any age.

6. Data Security

Since the current version of the App does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal information stored on our servers.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Memory Cards does not maintain personal information on external servers.

Game-related information stored locally on your device can be removed by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data through your device settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
